About Us

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who we are

DLTK.io is an independent website that publishes a free, browser-based TikTok video downloader and related guides. The site is operated by the DLTK team. We are not TikTok, ByteDance, or any company affiliated with them.

We built DLTK so people can save a TikTok video, soundtrack, cover image, or story they already have access to — without installing an app, creating an account, or paying a fee. The publisher of this site can be reached at info@dltk.io.

What this website does

The main product on DLTK.io is a set of online tools:

  • TikTok video downloader — paste a public TikTok link and save an MP4, including versions without the TikTok watermark when the source allows it.
  • TikTok to MP3 — extract the audio from a TikTok video as an MP3 file.
  • Thumbnail downloader — save the cover image of a TikTok video.
  • Story downloader — save a TikTok Story link from a desktop or phone browser.

These tools run in the browser. We do not ask for a TikTok username or password, we do not require registration, and we do not store the videos you download on our servers after the request is finished.

Who the site is for

DLTK is for people who want a copy of a TikTok they can already watch — for example creators archiving their own posts, students saving a clip for a project, or viewers keeping a video that may disappear from the app. The service is meant for personal, lawful use. Users are responsible for respecting copyright and TikTok’s rules. DLTK does not grant any extra rights to the videos you download.

Editorial content

Besides the tools, we publish how-to articles on the DLTK blog. Those guides explain, in plain language, how to download TikTok videos, convert them to MP3, or save them on a computer. We write and maintain that content ourselves so visitors can use the site without hunting through unrelated forums.

How the site is funded

DLTK.io is free to use. To cover hosting and keep the downloader available, we may display advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are labeled as advertising. Using the downloader does not require clicking an ad, and we do not sell your downloads or your personal files.

We also use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. Details are in our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
