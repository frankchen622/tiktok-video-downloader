Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2024

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit, making it easier to use and more useful.

How We Use Cookies

DLTK uses minimal cookies to ensure the website functions properly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that help maintain your session state
  • Security Cookies: Protect against security threats and ensure safe browsing

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website:

  • Page views and popular features
  • Browser type and device information
  • Geographic location (country/region level only)
  • Referral sources

These cookies collect anonymous data and cannot identify individual users.

Third-Party Cookies

We do not allow third-party cookies on our website. All cookies are set by DLTK only.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Disabling Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser, but this may affect website functionality. Essential features like the download form may not work properly without cookies.

Cookie Duration

Most cookies we use are session cookies that expire when you close your browser. Some may persist for up to 30 days to improve your experience on return visits.

Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals. If your browser sends a DNT signal, we will not collect analytics data from your visit.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect cookies from children.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy as our practices change. Check this page periodically for updates.
