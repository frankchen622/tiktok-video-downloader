Method 5: Screen Recording (Last Resort)

Best for: When other methods don't work

Screen recording captures whatever is displayed on your screen, including TikTok videos.

How to Screen Record:

  • iPhone/iPad: Swipe down Control Center → Tap record button
  • Android: Swipe down notification panel → Tap screen record
  • Windows: Windows + G to open Game Bar → Click record
  • Mac: Command + Shift + 5 → Select recording area
