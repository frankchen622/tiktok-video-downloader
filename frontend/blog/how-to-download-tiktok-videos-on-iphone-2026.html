How to Download TikTok Videos on iPhone (2026)

An iPhone will play a TikTok without a fuss, then act like the file does not exist. The Share menu has a dozen icons and still no obvious "save the real video" button. You do not need an App Store downloader to fix that, and you do not need to jailbreak the phone. Safari can take a public link and put an MP4 in Files. From there it is one extra tap to Photos.

This page stays on Safari, the Files app, and the camera roll. Windows and Mac are covered in the PC download guide, and a Samsung or Pixel belongs in the Android guide. The wider set of methods is in the complete download guide.

Why iPhone Downloads Feel Different

iOS keeps the file system out of sight, so a "saved" video can land in three different apps. People usually check only one of them.

  • Photos — the camera roll. A video shows up here only after you choose Save Video, or after TikTok's own save writes a copy.
  • Files — where Safari actually stores downloads. The folder is either On My iPhone → Downloads or iCloud Drive → Downloads, based on Settings → Safari → Downloads.
  • Inside the TikTok app — the built-in Save control. It is official, it adds a watermark, and many creators turn it off.

App Store "downloader" apps rarely add anything Safari cannot do. Some are just a website behind an icon, and a few ask you to install a configuration profile. Skip those. A Safari tab does not need a profile, a forum shortcut, or a jailbreak.

Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on an iPhone all browse with WebKit. They can open the same page, but their downloads are less consistent about landing in Files. If a file seems to vanish, repeat the steps in Safari before you blame the link.

Method 2: TikTok's Own Save Button

Best for: A quick personal copy when the watermark is fine

Tap Share and look for Save video. When the creator allows it, TikTok keeps a copy in the app and sometimes in Photos. The watermark stays on that copy. If the row is missing, the creator turned saves off. That is an app setting, not a fault in the iPhone.

How to Use:

  1. Open the video in the TikTok app and tap Share
  2. Tap Save video if it is listed
  3. Check Photos, and also your Saved collection inside TikTok

Method 3: Screen Recording from Control Center

Best for: A public video that plays, when the link itself will not save

Screen Recording films the glass, including the status bar, the like-button column, and any banner that slides in. The clip goes to Photos, but it is a recording of the player, not the original upload.

How to Screen Record:

  1. Open Settings → Control Center and add Screen Recording if the round button is missing
  2. Swipe down from the top-right corner, tap the record button, and wait out the three-second countdown
  3. Play the TikTok full screen. Do not tap anything else
  4. Tap the red status bar or the Control Center button again to stop
  5. The clip lands in Photos. Trim the start and end in the Photos editor

Leave the microphone off unless you want room noise in the clip. Do not record a private or friends-only video. If the post will not open while you are logged out, it is not available to download.

Method 4: Pass the Link to a Computer

Best for: Editing on a Mac, or an iPhone that is nearly full

If a Mac or Windows PC is nearby, send the link with AirDrop, Messages, or Mail and follow the PC steps. AirDrop the finished MP4 back only if you still want it on the phone. This keeps a tight camera roll clear and gives iMovie or CapCut a normal file to open.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone to download a TikTok?

No. Jailbreaking is not part of this process and it is not required. Safari can save a public video to Files without changing the phone's software.

Does Chrome on iPhone work, or do I have to use Safari?

Chrome can open the page, because every iOS browser uses WebKit. Safari is still the one to use when you want the file to show up reliably in the Files app. If Chrome's download list looks empty, repeat the paste in Safari.

Where did the video go after I tapped Download?

Check Files, not Photos, first. Look in On My iPhone → Downloads, or iCloud Drive → Downloads if Safari is set to store downloads in iCloud. The setting is under Settings → Safari → Downloads.

How do I move the download into the Photos app?

Open the MP4 in Files, tap the Share icon, and choose Save Video. Give it a second, then look in Photos → Recents. If Save Video is missing, the file may still be downloading or the phone may be out of space.

Will the saved video have a TikTok watermark?

Not when you save it through DLTK. TikTok's own Save video control keeps the watermark. Screen recordings can also catch the logo if it is on screen while you record.

Can I download private or friends-only TikToks on an iPhone?

No. If the account is private, or the post is limited to friends, it cannot be downloaded. Being able to watch it while you are logged in does not make it a public file. Do not look for a workaround.

The download failed and my storage looks full. What now?

Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage. Offload a few large apps or delete old screen recordings, then try again. Also confirm Safari has permission to store downloads on the phone rather than in an iCloud account that is itself full.

Is it legal to save a TikTok on my iPhone?

Saving a public video for personal viewing, or backing up a video you made, is the use these steps describe. The creator still owns the work. Do not re-upload someone else's video as your own, put it in an ad, or sell it without permission.
