How to Download a Douyin Video (and How It Differs from TikTok) (2026)

A Douyin link is not a TikTok link. ByteDance lists TikTok under global markets excluding China, and Douyin as a China-market product. TikTok's newsroom states that TikTok is not available in mainland China and does not operate there, and a TikTok newsroom statement describes Douyin as a separate application, with its own code and management teams, operated by different companies in different markets. DLTK is a TikTok video downloader. Paste a public TikTok URL into DLTK. A share link on v.douyin.com, douyin.com, or iesdouyin.com is a Douyin address, and DLTK does not treat it as a TikTok video. Private and friends-only posts are off limits. Keep a file for personal use or as a backup of your own work. Do not republish someone else's video without permission.

Two Products, Two Catalogs

ByteDance's products page puts TikTok in the global-markets column, excluding China, and Douyin in the China-market column (Products & Services). They are different products for different markets, in the same family of companies.

TikTok's newsroom says TikTok is not available in mainland China and does not operate there (The Truth About TikTok). A statement published in response to coverage in France says Douyin is a completely separate application from TikTok, with distinct code and different management teams, operated by different companies on different markets, and available only in China (TikTok newsroom statement).

That split is why a Douyin share link fails in a TikTok tool. The tool looks up a public TikTok object, and a Douyin video ID does not. Replacing the host does not create a tiktok.com URL. A Douyin profile is not a TikTok profile.

This article does not explain how to reach Douyin from outside its market. If a Douyin page does not load where you are, that is an availability limit, not a broken DLTK button.

Method 2: Pull a Clean URL Out of the Share Text

Best for: A message that looks like a paragraph, when you only need the address

Keep the https address. Words around the share card are not the link.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Paste the share text into a notes app. Do not type the address by hand; short-link codes can be case-sensitive
  2. Select only the address that starts with https:// and ends before the next space. If it was cut off in a chat, copy it again from the original share button
  3. Look at the host. For tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com, or vt.tiktok.com, use Method 1 on DLTK if the post is public. For v.douyin.com, douyin.com, or iesdouyin.com, stop: cleaning a Douyin URL does not convert it into a TikTok URL

✅ Pros:

  • Fixes leftover share text or a clipped URL
  • Works in any notes app

❌ Cons:

  • A clean Douyin URL is still a Douyin URL
  • A stale short link can still expire

Method 3: Use the Official Save Control in the App You Actually Have

Best for: A video you can already open in TikTok or in Douyin, when that app offers a save-to-device option

In the TikTok app, Save video is the first-party copy when the creator has not disabled it, and the file often includes TikTok's watermark. If Save is missing on a public post, paste that TikTok link into DLTK, as the downloader vs built-in guide explains.

In the Douyin app, use only the official save control Douyin shows, and only for content you are allowed to keep. This article does not provide unofficial extractors, modified apps, or steps for reaching Douyin from a region where it is not offered. A hidden control is a creator or platform setting, not a reason to record a private or friends-only post.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Confirm which app opened the link. Official controls apply only in that product
  2. If official save is offered and you have the right to keep a copy, use that control, then check Photos, Files, or Downloads
  3. If you are in TikTok, the post is public, and official Save is missing, go back to Method 1 with the TikTok URL. If you are in Douyin and official save is missing, stop there

✅ Pros:

  • The official file from the product you already use
  • No third-party tool

❌ Cons:

  • Official copies may include the platform watermark
  • The control can be disabled, and it will not appear for private content you should not download

Frequently Asked Questions

Can DLTK download a Douyin video?

No. DLTK is a TikTok downloader. Paste a public TikTok URL. A v.douyin.com, douyin.com, or iesdouyin.com link is not a TikTok link.

Why did a friend's short link fail on DLTK?

Check the host. If it is a Douyin short link, the tool is being asked for a different product, not a missing TikTok file. If it is a TikTok short link and the post is public, copy the URL again and paste only the https address.

Are Douyin and TikTok the same app with two names?

No. ByteDance lists them as different products for different markets, and TikTok's newsroom describes Douyin as a separate application with its own code and teams. TikTok is not available in mainland China.

If I have a Douyin account, can I open the same video on TikTok?

Not by assuming the catalogs are shared. A Douyin video lives on Douyin and a TikTok video lives on TikTok, so the same person posting on both has two links.

Can I just change the domain from douyin.com to tiktok.com?

No. The path and ID on a Douyin URL identify a Douyin object. Replacing the host does not create a valid TikTok post.

Can DLTK download private Douyin or private TikTok videos?

No. Private and friends-only posts are off limits on both products. Do not send a password to a downloader, and do not record the screen to bypass the setting.

Am I allowed to keep the file?

A backup of your own public post, or of a file you have the right to keep, fits these steps. Other people's videos and music remain theirs. Do not republish or sell them without permission. This page is a practical guide, not legal advice.

Should I install a special app or APK to get Douyin videos?

No. This guide does not recommend unknown-source installers, unofficial extractors, or workarounds for regional availability. For public TikTok videos, use the browser on dltk.io. Android users who want a public TikTok file can stay in Chrome, as the Android guide explains.
