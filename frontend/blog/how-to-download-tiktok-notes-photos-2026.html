How to Save Photos from TikTok Notes (vs TikTok Photo Posts) (2026)

TikTok Notes is not a TikTok photo post. A 2024 TikTok newsroom article describes Notes as a dedicated app, tested in select markets, for photo and text, and describes photo posts in the main TikTok app as multi-slide carousels since 2022 (Enhancing Photo Sharing for TikTok Creators with Lemon8). DLTK is a TikTok video downloader. Paste a public TikTok URL into DLTK. A TikTok Notes link is not a TikTok video URL, and DLTK does not treat it as one. If the post is a public photo carousel in the main TikTok app, use TikTok's official Save photo control when the creator allows it (Download content). DLTK can keep a cover from that public TikTok link; it does not export every slide, a ZIP, or a compiled slideshow MP4. Private and friends-only posts are off limits. Keep a file for personal use or as a backup of your own work. Do not republish someone else's photos without permission. In April 2025, TechCrunch reported that TikTok notified Notes users the standalone app would stop on May 8, 2025, and quoted a TikTok spokesperson about taking feedback from Notes to Lemon8 (TechCrunch). This 2026 guide does not treat Notes as a current, working download source. If a leftover Notes link does not open, a TikTok downloader cannot recover it.

Method 2: Use TikTok's Official Save Photo Control

Best for: Original stills from a public main-app photo post, when the creator allows them

TikTok's help page on downloading content says to open the post, tap Share, then Save photo. Select all or individual stills. If Save photo is missing, the creator has not allowed downloads. Private posts cannot be saved this way.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stay in the main TikTok app, not on a leftover Notes page. Save photo applies to a carousel when the creator leaves it on. It does not turn a dead Notes link into a TikTok carousel.
  2. Use Save photo only when the public post offers it. Tap Share, then Save photo. Check Photos, the TikTok album, Files, or Downloads, and open each file.
  3. If Save photo is missing, stop or return to Method 1 with a public TikTok URL. Do not record the screen or log into someone else's account.

✅ Pros:

  • The official stills from the app, when the creator allows them
  • No third-party tool

❌ Cons:

  • The control can be turned off, and it will not appear for private content
  • It does not save a standalone Notes post that is no longer available

Method 3: Do Not Treat a Screenshot, or a Domain Swap, as a Notes Download

Best for: A leftover Notes link that fails, or a screen capture instead of the file

A screenshot shows the player, not the source photo, and it does not bypass privacy settings. Changing a Notes or photos host to tiktok.com does not create a valid post, because the path still points at a different object.

  1. If the link does not open, that is a closed product path, not a DLTK failure. TikTok's newsroom described Notes as a test in select markets.   3. Keep only what you have the right to keep. Use Method 2 or Method 1 for your own public TikTok photo post. Do not republish or sell other people's photos or music without permission.
