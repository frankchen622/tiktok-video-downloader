How to Download a TikTok Photo Slideshow (Images, ZIP, or MP4) (2026)

A TikTok photo slideshow is a photo post: still images in one carousel, often with music. TikTok calls this Photo Mode. The Share link can contain /video/, and a song can play, but the files inside are pictures. Save original stills with TikTok's Save photo control when the creator allows it. DLTK can keep one cover from that public link, and it does not return the slides, a ZIP, or a compiled slideshow MP4. A compiled MP4 exists when the photos were published as a video, or when you build one later from images you already saved. A ZIP is an archive you make yourself. Method 1 is the check. Private and friends-only posts are off limits throughout.

A Video, a Photo Post, and TikTok Notes

A regular video post is one file: frames, a duration, and a soundtrack. The MP4 guide covers that object.

Photo Mode is the carousel. In October 2022, TikTok described still images that display one after another, with optional music, while viewers swipe (editing tools announcement). Its help page lists Photo beside video and text, and allows up to 35 photos in one post (how to make a post). That figure is a posting limit, not a download count.

TikTok Notes is a separate app. A 2024 newsroom post describes it as a test, in select markets, for photo and text, and describes main-app photo posts as multi-slide carousels since 2022 (photo sharing update). This guide does not claim DLTK can save Notes.

Some slideshows are videos. A 2020 TikTok tutorial shows uploading images from the record screen so they play as a video (photos tutorial). That file is a video. Photo Mode leaves stills you swipe. The Share link can look the same.

Three Files People Think They Already Have

A screenshot is a picture of the glass. The status bar, like column, caption, username, and slide dots sit in the frame, and the photo is cropped around them. It is a reminder, not the still the creator uploaded.

Original stills are the image files in the post. TikTok's help page says Save photo saves them when the creator allows downloads. With several photos, select all or pick some, then tap Save. A missing control means the creator blocked it (download content). The files land in the camera roll as separate photos, not a ZIP, without the on-screen buttons drawn on them.

A compiled video is a new MP4: stills in sequence, plus music. Photo Mode does not include that file. A video made from photos does, and DLTK can save it. An editor can sequence stills you already saved. That export is a new encode, and a commercial sound has its own copyright.

Why a Tool Says "This Is Not a Video"

That message is a file-type check. Photo posts use the same /video/ path as clips, including short vm and vt links, so the URL is a weak clue. A profile URL is a different failure, covered in the why-it-failed guide.

A video changes frames the whole time. A photo post holds a still until it advances or you swipe, usually with slide dots. Music can play throughout. If you can swipe back to an earlier image, you are in a photo post.

The downloader then asks for a video stream and finds images instead. DLTK's parser takes the music address when that is the only playable format, hides those audio-only entries, and still shows Download Video. You do not get one button per slide. "Could not parse video," on a link that shows stills, means the tool wanted a video and the post had none. Private, friends-only, and deleted posts are a separate stop.

Method 2: Save the Original Photos in the TikTok App

Best for: The actual stills, when the creator allows downloads

TikTok documents these steps on its download help page.

  1. Open the photo post in the TikTok app.
  2. Tap Share, then tap Save photo.
  3. For several photos, tap Select all or tap the ones you want, then tap Save.

The images land in the camera roll. A missing Save photo option means the creator blocked downloads. On iPhone that save belongs in Photos. A DLTK cover follows the browser folder in the iPhone guide.

Method 3: Screenshot the Slide You Can Already See

Best for: A public post, when Save photo is missing and a rough copy is enough

The buttons are in the picture because they are on the screen. Swipe, then shoot again for the next frame. Do this only on a public post you can already watch.

Method 4: A ZIP or a Compiled MP4, After You Have the Files

Frequently Asked Questions

Can DLTK download every photo in a slideshow?

No. Save Thumbnail keeps the single cover when it appears. The other images need TikTok's Save photo, and only if the creator allows it.

Where is the ZIP?

DLTK does not make one. Save photo writes images to the camera roll. A ZIP is an archive of files already on your device.

Can I get one MP4 with the photos and the music?

Only when the post is already a video. DLTK will not compile Photo Mode stills. The MP3 page is the sound alone, on a public post.

Why does the link say /video/ if the post is photos?

TikTok uses that path for photo posts too. If the image holds still and slide marks appear, treat it as a photo post.

How is TikTok Notes different from a photo post?

Notes is a separate app TikTok tested in select markets. This page does not cover saving it.

A site says "this is not a video." Is the link dead?

Often the post is a carousel and the link still opens. If TikTok itself errors, the post may be gone. See the troubleshooting guide.

Can I save a private or friends-only slideshow?

No. If a logged-out browser cannot open it, it is not a public file. Do not send a password to a downloader, and do not record the screen to bypass the setting.

Am I allowed to keep the photos?

Personal viewing, and a backup of your own post, fit these steps. The creator still owns the photos and the music. Do not republish or sell them without permission.
