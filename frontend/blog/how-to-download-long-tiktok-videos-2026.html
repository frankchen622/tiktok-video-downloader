How to Download Long TikTok Videos (10-Minute Recordings and 60-Minute Uploads) (2026)

A long TikTok is still one public post, with one link. Official Camera tools help in TikTok's Help Center says videos you record in TikTok can be up to 10 minutes long, and videos you upload in TikTok can be up to 60 minutes long. The same page says that if you select a sound before you record or upload, the video length will be determined by the sound length. To save a public long video, paste that public http(s) TikTok link into DLTK (https://dltk.io/) and wait until the browser finishes writing the file. Then open the file's properties and check the duration — a progress bar is not proof the transfer completed. A photo slideshow, or a row of short clips from the same creator, is not the same as one long video; each object has its own link and its own file. DLTK cannot download private or friends-only posts. Keep a file for personal use or as a backup of your own work. Do not republish someone else's video or music without permission.

Method 2: Confirm You Have One Long Video, Not a Slideshow or a Series

Best for: Avoiding a failed save that was never a single long MP4

Official Camera tools help covers how long a recording or an upload can be. It does not turn a photo post into a 10-minute video, and it does not merge several short posts into one file.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Separate a long video from a photo slideshow. A photo post is images, often with music. Several minutes of playback still means pictures, not one long camera recording. If the player advances through stills, use the photo slideshow guide. That link will not produce a long talking-head MP4.
  2. Separate one post from a series of short clips. Each public video has its own URL. DLTK saves one public link at a time and does not bundle clips into one file. Copy each link and save each file. Do not invent a “download this account” step.
  3. Use the official length rules to set expectations. Official Camera tools help says a recording can run up to 10 minutes and an upload up to 60 minutes. A sound selected first sets the length, so a 30-second official sound will not become a 10-minute recording. Those figures are creation limits, not a promise that every public file is still available, and not a DLTK duration cap. Private and friends-only posts stay off limits.

✅ Pros:

  • You avoid pasting the wrong kind of link
  • Official length rules explain why some posts can exceed 10 minutes

❌ Cons:

  • You still have to inspect the post; a long playback time is not always one video file

Method 3: Why Official Save and a Screen Recording Are Weaker for Long Videos

Best for: A quick keepsake when you do not need a clean master file

TikTok's official Download content help covers saving in the app when the creator allows it. That save may add a watermark and a second, compressed encode — not a file whose duration and resolution you chose to inspect.

A screen recording captures your display, the player chrome, and any notification, at screen resolution rather than the upload's. A locked phone, low battery, or an interruption can cut it short. Record the screen only when a public video plays and will not download. Do not use it on private or friends-only posts. When the logo does not matter, official Save is enough. For a clean public file, use Method 1.

✅ Pros:

  • Official Save stays inside TikTok when the creator allows it
  • A screen recording can capture something you can already play in public

❌ Cons:

  • Official Save often includes a watermark and a second compression
  • A screen recording is a new file of your display, not a verified copy of the source duration and resolution

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a TikTok video be?

Official Camera tools help says videos you record in TikTok can be up to 10 minutes long, and videos you upload in TikTok can be up to 60 minutes long. Those are creation limits on TikTok's official help page. They can change if TikTok updates the article, and they do not mean every public post is still available to save.

Why does my recording stop earlier than 10 minutes?

The same official page says that if you select a sound before you record or upload, the video length is determined by the sound length. A short official sound caps the post at that sound's length. This is a creation rule, not a downloader error.

The video feels long, but I did not get one long MP4. Why?

It may not be one video. A photo slideshow is still images, often with music. A long session on a profile is often many short posts, each with its own link. DLTK saves one public URL at a time. Use the slideshow guide for photo posts, and copy each video's link if you are saving a series.

How do I check the duration of the file I saved?

On a computer, use Properties, Get Info, or a player's information panel. On iPhone, open the file in Files and look at the info panel. On Android, a player such as VLC from the Play Store can show the length. Compare that number with the public post. This guide does not invent typical file sizes.

Why did the download take a long time, or look unfinished?

A longer video is a larger transfer. Wait until the browser marks the download complete before you open the file. Can I download a private or friends-only long TikTok?

No. Private and friends-only posts are off limits, regardless of how long they are. A long runtime does not change that.
