How to Download TikTok Videos Online for Free (2026)

You can download a public TikTok in the browser you already have. No App Store install, no Play Store install, no account to create, and no desktop program to keep updated. You copy a link, paste it on a website, and save the file. That is the whole idea of doing it online.

Why Download Online Instead of Installing Something

An install hangs around. A browser tab does not. That matters on a shared computer, a school Chromebook, or a phone you do not want filled with one-purpose apps. A website also cannot change its mind and keep Accessibility access after you are done. You close the tab.

  • No store review and no update cycle — you are not waiting on an app release to save one video
  • No sign-up — a free online tool should not need an account before it will fetch a public link
  • No leftover permissions — nothing stays installed when you leave
  • Same steps on a phone or a computer — the browser is the app

Free, here, means you do not pay for software and you do not create an account on DLTK to run the download. It does not mean every video on the internet is yours to republish.

Method 2: How This Compares with Apps and Desktop Software

Best for: Deciding you do not need them

A mobile app can feel faster if you save many videos a day, and it costs you a permanent install, notifications, and storage permission. Desktop software adds an installer and, on Windows, the habit of running a program from a random download page. For one public video, or even a handful, the website does the same fetch without those leftovers.

If an online page tells you the browser method failed and the repair is an APK or an .exe, treat that as a different product. Go back to the plain page instead of following the install prompt.

Method 3: TikTok's Own Save, Which Is Not a Website

Best for: A watermarked copy inside the app

Official Save video is an in-app control, not an online download. It needs your TikTok account, it keeps a watermark, and creators can turn it off. It is fine for a personal copy of your own post. It is not the "paste a link in a browser" workflow this guide is about. Use it when you are already in the app and the logo does not matter.

Method 4: How to Pick a Safe Free Online Tool

Best for: Any site, not only this one

Free online tools vary. Before you paste a link somewhere new, check the page against a short list:

  • The address is HTTPS and it is the site you meant to open
  • It does not ask for your TikTok username or password
  • It does not require a browser extension, a configuration profile, or an APK
  • It does not put a survey, a "human check" that installs software, or an account wall in front of a public video
  • You can leave without creating a login

DLTK is built to pass that list: a page, a link, a file. If a lookalike site adds a password field, you are no longer on this workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up to download online?

Not on DLTK. Open the page, paste a public link, and save the file. A site that blocks the download behind a new account is adding a step this workflow does not need.

Is an online downloader actually free?

DLTK does not charge for the download and does not sell a premium unlock for it. You still need your own internet connection. "Free" is not a promise about every other website using the same words.

Does online mean no watermark?

Not by definition. "Online" means the browser, with no install. The file DLTK returns does not include the TikTok watermark. If the watermark is the only thing you care about, the no-watermark guide is the page written for that.

Can I do this on a phone and a computer?

Yes. The page is the same. Phones put the file in Files or Downloads. Computers put it in the Downloads folder. The PC guide covers Windows and Mac folder details.

What should a free online tool never ask for?

Your TikTok password, a configuration profile, an extension install, or an APK. Pasting a public link is the whole request. Anything beyond that is a reason to leave.

Can I download private videos online for free?

No. Private and friends-only videos are not available, free or otherwise. An online tool that claims otherwise is not telling the truth. Do not paste passwords trying to make it work.

The page loaded, but nothing saved. What failed?

Often the in-app browser, a blocked download, or a link that is not a public video. Open Chrome or Safari directly, allow downloads, and copy the link again from Share. Photo posts and expired stories will not become video files. The photo slideshow guide explains that case.

Am I allowed to save videos this way?

Personal viewing and backups of your own public posts are what these steps are for. The creator still owns the work. Do not republish, advertise, or sell someone else's video without permission.
