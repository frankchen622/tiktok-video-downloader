How to Download TikTok Videos on Android (2026)

Android will save a TikTok, but only if you treat it like a file download and not like an app install. Chrome already has a download manager. The video belongs in the Files folder. You do not need an APK, a new launcher icon, or a switch labeled Install unknown apps.

This page is the Android path: Chrome, the notification shade, and the Downloads folder. iPhone steps live in the iPhone guide. A Windows or Mac computer is covered in the PC guide. For the wider set of options, use the complete download guide and come back here when the phone itself is the question.

Why Android Is Its Own Case

Android phones do not share one Files app. A Pixel shows Google Files. A Samsung phone shows My Files. Xiaomi, Oppo, and others ship their own browser and their own folder names. The download still lands in the same kind of place: a Downloads directory on internal storage.

That is also why random "TikTok downloader" APKs are a bad trade. Installing one means allowing that source to install unknown apps, then giving it storage access it does not need for a single public video. Play Protect can flag these packages, and an app that asks for Accessibility can read the screen long after the download. A Chrome tab does not ask for any of that.

Method 2: TikTok's Built-in Save

Best for: A watermarked copy of your own post

Inside the TikTok app, Share sometimes includes Save video. When the creator allows it, the app stores a watermarked copy. That is a fair way to keep your own upload close by. It is a poor way to get a clean file for an edit, and the button is often absent. A missing button means the creator disabled it. Installing another app will not turn that official switch back on.

How to Use:

  1. Open the video and tap Share
  2. Tap Save video if you see it
  3. Look in TikTok's saved collection, and in Photos or Gallery if the app also wrote a file there

Method 3: Why Third-Party APKs Are the Wrong Fix

Best for: Understanding what not to install

Search results for "TikTok downloader APK" point at sites that are not the Play Store. The file is an installer. Android will block it until you allow that browser or file manager to install unknown apps. After it is on the phone, the app may ask for storage, display over other apps, or Accessibility.

None of those permissions are required to save one public video in Chrome. If a page tells you the download failed and the only fix is an APK, close the tab. You can review Install unknown apps later under Settings → Apps → Special app access, and turn the toggle off for any browser you do not want installing packages.

✅ Pros:

  • There is no real upside versus a browser download

❌ Cons:

  • Malware and ad-heavy installers are common in this category
  • Accessibility access can outlive the download
  • Updates arrive from outside the Play Store

Method 4: Screen Record from Quick Settings

Best for: A public video that plays when the link will not save

Swipe down twice to open Quick Settings and tap Screen record. The name and icon vary by brand. Start the recording, play the TikTok, then stop from the notification. The clip is saved to Gallery or Movies. It includes whatever was on screen, and it is softer than a real file download.

Use this only for a public video you can already watch. It is not a way to keep a private account or a friends-only post. If the video will not open while you are logged out, stop there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the video after Chrome says it downloaded?

Open the Files or My Files app and go to Downloads. You can also tap the completed item in the notification shade. Chrome's own Downloads list (Menu → Downloads) points at the same file.

Do I need to turn on unknown sources?

No. A browser download is not an app install. If a site tells you to allow Install unknown apps before the video will save, close it. That toggle is for APKs, not for MP4 files.

Samsung Internet instead of Chrome — does that matter?

No. Paste the same link. Samsung Internet also saves to the Downloads folder, which My Files can open. The steps after the paste are the same.

Can I download a private TikTok on Android?

No. Private accounts and friends-only posts cannot be downloaded with DLTK or with the other methods here. Watching the video while you are logged in does not make it public.

The official Save button is missing. Is the video blocked?

Not always. Creators can disable TikTok's own save and still leave the video public. A public link can still be pasted into DLTK. If the video page itself will not open, the link is not a normal public post.

Will the file have a watermark?

A DLTK download of a public video does not keep the TikTok watermark. The in-app Save video control does. A screen recording keeps whatever was visible, including a logo on screen.

Can I move the MP4 to an SD card?

Yes, after it is in Downloads. In Files, long-press the video and choose Move. Pick the SD card if the phone has one mounted. Do that yourself in Files rather than granting a random downloader broad storage access.

Is saving a TikTok on my phone allowed?

These steps are for personal viewing and for backing up videos you created. The creator keeps the rights. Do not repost someone else's video as your own, use it in ads, or sell it without permission.
