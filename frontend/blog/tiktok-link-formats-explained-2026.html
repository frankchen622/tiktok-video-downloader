Every TikTok Link Format Explained (vm, vt, Desktop, and TikTok Lite) (2026)

A usable TikTok link is a complete public http(s) address that opens one post — one video, one photo post, or one story — not a profile, a sound page, or a hashtag. Official Sharing help tells you to open the post, tap Share, and choose how to share it. Copy link in that menu often produces a short TikTok host such as vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com, or a /t/ path on tiktok.com. In a desktop browser, the same public post usually appears as https://www.tiktok.com/@username/video/ followed by the post ID; a photo post may use /photo/ instead of /video/. A complete short link and a complete desktop link can open the same public post. A truncated message link or a profile URL is not a complete post link. TikTok Lite is an official app; official Lite help says account data, privacy settings, and posted content apply across TikTok and TikTok Lite. Copy the post link the same way. Paste that complete public address into DLTK (https://dltk.io/) and wait until the browser finishes writing the file. DLTK cannot download private or friends-only posts. Keep a file for personal use or as a backup of your own work. Do not republish someone else's video or music without permission.

What Each Link Format Actually Is

Official Sharing help does not publish a catalog of hosts. Logged out, a playing post is a post link.

Full desktop (and web) post URLs

A video on tiktok.com looks like https://www.tiktok.com/@username/video/ plus the post ID. A photo post may use /photo/ instead of /video/. Without a /video/ or /photo/ ID, https://www.tiktok.com/@username is a profile. Official Sharing help shares a profile separately.

Short share links: vm, vt, and /t/

Copy link often returns vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com, or a /t/ path on tiktok.com. Official help does not map vm or vt to a device, an operating system, or a region, and this guide does not invent one. Paste that complete short link only if it still opens the public post, and do not rewrite a damaged string.

TikTok Lite links

Official TikTok Lite help describes an official app for less cellular data, with a similar experience and limited features. It currently says TikTok Lite is only available on Android devices — check that article and the store listing in your country. With your existing login, account data, privacy settings, and posted content apply across both apps. Use Share → Copy link. This guide does not invent a Lite-only host. Paste the address when that complete public http(s) link opens the post.

Addresses that are not a post

Official Sharing help has separate steps for a profile, a sound, and a hashtag. Those pages are not one video file. v.douyin.com and douyin.com are Douyin, a different product — see the Douyin guide. A preview or caption with no URL is not a link.

Why a text-message link fails

A message may cut the path, insert an ellipsis, or paste a title with the URL. Do not guess missing characters. See the troubleshooting guide for a private post, an expired story, or a missing file.

Method 2: Open the Link in a Browser, Then Copy the Full Post URL

Best for: A chat link that might be damaged, or when you want the desktop URL before you download

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Paste the address into Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox — not an in-app preview window. Include https://.
  2. Stay logged out. If the public post plays, continue. A profile, sound, hashtag, or error means copy the link from the post.
  3. Copy the address bar into DLTK and wait until the file finishes.

✅ Pros:

  • Shows whether the text is a post, a profile, or a broken fragment, and recovers the desktop URL

❌ Cons:

  • An extra step when the short link was already complete. It still cannot open private or friends-only posts, and it will not bypass a blocked tiktok.com page

Method 3: Save in the TikTok App When the Creator Allows It

Best for: Keeping a copy inside TikTok's official download flow, when that button is available

Official Download content help explains saving a video or photo in the TikTok app when the creator allows it. You cannot download private videos. The file may include the TikTok watermark and the creator's username.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Open the post in the TikTok app
  2. Tap Share, then Download, Save video, or Save photo. Official help uses these labels, and only posts with downloads turned on show the option
  3. Check your gallery or the TikTok album

✅ Pros:

  • Uses TikTok's own save option, with no URL to paste elsewhere

❌ Cons:

  • The button is missing when downloads are off. The file may include a watermark and username, and private videos still cannot be downloaded

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com?

Official Sharing help does not define one, and this page does not invent a device or region rule. Either complete address is a short post link when it opens that public post.

Do I need the full /@username/video/ URL, or will a short link work?

Paste a complete short link into DLTK. If it is cut off, copy it again or use the address bar.

Why did a link from a text message fail?

It may have been trimmed, split, or pasted with a title. Paste only the https:// URL, and do not guess the missing part.

Is a TikTok Lite link different from a regular TikTok link?

Official Lite help says posted content applies across both apps. This guide does not invent a Lite-only host.

Can I paste a profile URL into DLTK?

No. A profile is an account, not one post. Copy the video or photo link.

What about a Douyin link a friend sent me?

Douyin is a different product. v.douyin.com is not a TikTok URL. See the Douyin guide.

Can I download a private or friends-only post if I have the link?

No. Official Download content help says private videos cannot be downloaded, and DLTK cannot save friends-only posts either.

Am I allowed to keep the file I save?

Keep your own post, or a file you may keep. Do not republish someone else's video or music. This is not legal advice.
