Method 4: Screen Record, Then Extract Audio (Last Resort)

Best for: When the video will not convert

Screen recording captures whatever plays on your device, including the sound. You then strip the audio in an editor. Use this only if the link will not convert — quality is lower, and you may pick up UI sounds.

How to Screen Record:

  • iPhone — Control Center → Screen Recording
  • Android — Quick settings → Screen record
  • Windows — Windows + G → Record
  • Mac — Command + Shift + 5

✅ Pros:

  • Works even when other tools fail
  • Built into most devices

❌ Cons:

  • Lower audio quality
  • Large video files before you extract MP3
  • Time-consuming
  • May include notification sounds
