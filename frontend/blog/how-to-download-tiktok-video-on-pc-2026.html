Method 2: TikTok Website (With Watermark)

Best for: A quick save when the watermark is acceptable

tiktok.com lets some videos be saved from a desktop browser after you sign in. The official save still adds a TikTok watermark, and many creators turn the option off.

How to Use:

  1. Open tiktok.com in Chrome, Edge, or Safari and sign in
  2. Find the video (or paste the link into the address bar)
  3. Click Share → Save video, if the button is available
  4. Check your Downloads folder

✅ Pros:

  • Official TikTok website
  • No third-party tool
  • Works in a normal desktop browser

❌ Cons:

  • Includes a TikTok watermark
  • Requires a TikTok account
  • Save is often disabled by the creator
  • Quality can be lower than the original upload

Method 3: Browser Extensions

Best for: People who download from tiktok.com all day

Chrome and Firefox extensions can add a download button next to videos on the TikTok website. They are convenient, but they also run inside your browser — treat them like software you install.

What to watch for:

  • Install only from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons, never from a random .exe site
  • Read recent reviews and the permission list — skip anything that wants your TikTok password
  • One-click downloaders that "update" outside the store are a common malware path

✅ Pros:

  • Download without leaving tiktok.com
  • Fast if you save many videos in a row

❌ Cons:

  • Can slow the browser down
  • Privacy and malware risk
  • Breaks when TikTok changes its site
  • Does not help if you only have a phone link

Method 4: Screen Recording (Last Resort)

Best for: When the link will not download

Screen recording captures whatever plays on the monitor, including the TikTok player. Quality is lower than a real MP4 download, and you may pick up UI chrome or notification sounds. Use this only as a fallback.

How to Screen Record:

  • Windows — Press Windows + G to open Xbox Game Bar, then click Record. Or use Win + Alt + R.
  • Mac — Press Command + Shift + 5, choose Record Selected Portion, and crop to the video.
  • Any PC — OBS Studio can record a window, but it is overkill for a single clip.

✅ Pros:

  • Built into Windows and macOS
  • Works even when other tools fail

❌ Cons:

  • Lower quality than a direct download
  • Large files
  • Easy to capture the mouse, captions, or ads
  • Takes more time than pasting a link
