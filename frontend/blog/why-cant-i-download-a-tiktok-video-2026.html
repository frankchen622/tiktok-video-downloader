Why Can't I Download a TikTok Video? Fixes (2026)

When a TikTok will not download, the link is usually the problem, or the post was never a public video. The fix is a short checklist, not a new app. Work from the top. Most failures sort themselves out before you change a setting.

Device steps, if you get that far, are in the iPhone guide, the Android guide, and the PC guide. If you are deciding between TikTok's button and a downloader, read downloader vs built-in save.

Check These Before You Retry

  1. Can you open the video in a browser while logged out? If not, it is not a normal public post.
  2. Did you copy a link, or a caption that merely contains the word TikTok?
  3. Is it a video, or a photo carousel, a live that was never posted, or a story that already expired?
  4. Does the page load on your network at all? A blocked tiktok.com page cannot be fetched as a file.
  5. Is the phone or computer out of storage, or did the browser block the download?

If the logged-out page plays, continue with Method 1. If it does not, skip ahead to the cause that matches. Do not try to force a private video through a tool. DLTK cannot download those, and neither can the other methods here.

The Usual Causes, and the Fix That Matches

The creator turned off TikTok's Save button

Share → Save video is missing on a lot of public posts. That disables the official copy. It does not always mean every other method fails. If you can watch the video logged out, paste the link into DLTK. The difference between the built-in button and a downloader is spelled out in the comparison guide.

The account is private, or the video is friends-only

There is no fix. If a logged-out browser cannot play it, it is not a public file. DLTK will not download it. Do not screen-record it to get around the setting, and do not look for a tool that claims it can. Friends-only is the same rule as private.

The link is incomplete or points at the wrong thing

A profile URL, a search page, or a caption with no https address will not resolve to one video. Copy link again from the post. Short vt.tiktok.com links are fine. A link that was cut off by a text message, with no end, is not.

It is a photo post, a live, or an expired story

Photo carousels are pictures, not one MP4. A livestream that was never published as a video has no ordinary video file. Stories disappear. If a story is still up, use the story saver while the link works. After it expires, the URL is dead for everyone.

The video was deleted, or your network cannot open TikTok

A removed post returns an error in the app and in a downloader. The same is true when tiktok.com will not load on your connection. DLTK can only fetch a page your network can reach. Switching browsers does not repair a dead or blocked URL. Age-restricted posts that demand a login are not normal public links either.

The browser saved it, and you are looking in the wrong app

iPhone users should check Files → Downloads before Photos. Android users should check the notification shade and the Downloads folder. Desktop users should check the browser's download shelf. The iPhone and Android guides show the exact folders. A full disk or a blocked pop-up produces the same "nothing happened" feeling.

When Another Method Is Worth Trying

Only after the checklist

  • Another browser — if Safari or Chrome blocked the file, try the other one on the same public link
  • A computer — the PC guide helps when phone storage or an in-app browser is the snag
  • Screen recording — last resort for a public video that plays on screen and still will not save as a file. It is a softer copy, and it is not for private posts
  • Stop — private, friends-only, deleted, expired, or never-posted live videos are done. A different downloader will not change that

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no download button on the TikTok itself?

The creator can turn off the official Save control. The video can still be public. Copy the link and try DLTK. If the video page will not open without an account, it is restricted and you should leave it.

Can DLTK download private videos if I can see them?

No. Seeing a post while logged into a private or friends-only account does not make it downloadable. DLTK only handles public videos. There is no setting that changes that.

The link worked yesterday. Why not today?

The creator may have deleted it, turned the account private, or the story may have expired. Open the link in a logged-out browser. If TikTok itself shows an error, the file is gone.

It is a slideshow of photos with music. Can I still save an MP4?

Often no. Those posts are images plus audio, not one video file. Save the images another way, or use the MP3 tool only if you wanted the sound and the post is public.

A live just ended. Where is the download?

Only if the creator posted a replay as a normal video. A live that exists only while it is streaming does not leave a standard video link to paste.

Could my browser be the problem?

Yes. In-app browsers inside TikTok, Instagram, or a mail app drop downloads. Open Safari or Chrome directly. Also check that pop-ups and downloads are allowed, and that the disk is not full.

Will a VPN or a different downloader get a private video?

No. Network tricks do not turn a private post into a public file. If the honest cause is privacy, deletion, or an expired story, switch tools and you will get the same result.

Is it okay to keep trying on a video I am allowed to watch?

For a public video, yes — a fresh copy of the link is reasonable. For personal viewing or your own backup, that is the use these steps describe. Do not republish someone else's video without permission, and do not keep hammering a private account.
