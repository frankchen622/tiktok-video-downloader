Can You Download TikTok Stories by Username, and Do You Appear in the Viewer List? (2026)

A TikTok username is not a Story URL. DLTK is a TikTok downloader: paste a public http(s) TikTok link, not an @handle. It will not pull every current Story from a profile name. Official help describes Stories as photos or videos you can watch for 24 hours, from the Inbox, public profiles, the Following feed, and the For You feed (Watching Stories on TikTok). The same page states that people can see you viewed their Story even if your account is private or your profile view history is turned off. If you posted the Story, official help says you can see the total views and swipe up to see the accounts that watched it (TikTok Stories). Profile view history is a separate optional setting: you only see visits from accounts that also have it on, from the past 30 days, and only if you are 16 or older with fewer than 5,000 followers. It is off by default, and turning it off does not hide a Story view (Profile view history). If you have a public Story link and the Story is still live, paste that URL into DLTK or the story saver. After 24 hours the Story is no longer available to watch, and a dead link cannot be recovered. Private and friends-only Stories are off limits. Keep a file for personal use or as a backup of your own work. Do not republish someone else's Story without permission. This guide does not cover username scrapers, unofficial "anonymous viewers," or ways to stay off a creator's viewer list.

Method 2: Use Official TikTok to Watch a Story, or to See Who Viewed Yours

Best for: What TikTok itself records — not a username downloader

Official viewing and official view lists are first-party features. They are not a "download every Story from this username" tool, and they are not a way to hide a view.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Watch a Story only where official help says you can. Use the Inbox, public profiles, the Following feed, or the For You feed (Watching Stories on TikTok). Opening a public profile to watch a live Story is viewing, not a bulk export. A reply or an emoji reaction is a message, not a file download.
  2. If the Story is yours, use the official viewer list. Tap your profile photo at the top of the Following feed, the Inbox, or your profile. Open the Story. The total is at the bottom. Swipe up to see the accounts that watched it (TikTok Stories).
  3. Do not confuse Story views with profile view history. People can see you viewed their Story even if your account is private or profile view history is off. That setting is separate: mutual on, past 30 days, 16 or older, fewer than 5,000 followers, and off by default (Profile view history). Turning it off does not hide a Story view, and a downloader is not a "hide me" switch.

❌ Cons:

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download TikTok Stories by username?

No. A username is not a Story URL. DLTK expects a public http(s) TikTok link. It will not pull every current Story from a profile name.

If I watch a Story in the TikTok app, do I appear in the viewer list?

Official watching help says people can see you viewed their Story even if your account is private or your profile view history is turned off. If you posted the Story, official help says you can see the total views and swipe up to see the accounts that watched it.

If I turn off profile view history, will I stay off the Story list?

No. Official help treats profile view history as a separate setting, with its own age, follower, and mutual-on limits. It also states that a Story view can still be visible when that setting is off.

Can I download private or friends-only Stories?

No. Private and friends-only Stories are off limits. Do not send a password to a downloader, and do not record the screen to bypass the setting.
