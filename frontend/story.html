Story Downloader FAQ

What are TikTok stories?
TikTok stories are temporary posts that disappear after 24 hours, similar to Instagram or Snapchat stories.
Can I download someone else's story?
Yes, if the story is publicly visible. Private or restricted stories cannot be accessed.
Will they know I downloaded their story?
No, DLTK downloads stories anonymously. The creator won't receive any notification.
How long do stories last?
TikTok stories disappear 24 hours after being posted. Download them before they're gone.
Is this legal?
Downloading stories for personal viewing is generally acceptable. Never redistribute or use content without the creator's permission.
