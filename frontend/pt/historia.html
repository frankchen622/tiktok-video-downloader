Understanding TikTok Story Feature

Everything you need to know about TikTok's 24-hour stories feature.

TikTok Stories is a feature that allows creators to share temporary vídeos that disappear after 24 hours, similar to Instagram Stories and Snapchat. Here's what makes TikTok stories unique:

What are TikTok Stories?

TikTok Stories are short vertical vídeos (up to 15 seconds) that appear at the top of followers' feeds and disappear automatically after 24 hours. Unlike regular TikTok vídeos, stories are temporary and don't appear on your profile permanently.

  • Research & Analysis - Salve stories for market research, competitor analysis, or trend documentation.

Story vs Regular TikTok Vídeos

Stories: Temporary (24 hours), appear at top of feed, can't be liked or shared publicly, more casual and spontaneous.

Regular Vídeos: Permanent (unless deleted), appear in main feed, full engagement features, more polished and produced.
